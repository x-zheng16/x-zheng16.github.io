About

I am a Research Assistant Professor at the Hong Kong Institute of AI for Science (HKAI-Sci), City University of Hong Kong, and work closely with Prof. Xingjun Ma at the Institute of Trustworthy Embodied AI, Fudan University. My research develops robust and efficient reinforcement-learning algorithms for trustworthy science, computer-use, and embodied agents.

I also collaborate closely with industry: my research has contributed to ByteDance’s AgentArmor agent-safety framework, Foxconn Research Institute’s FoxBrain foundation model (with NVIDIA), and HKAI-Sci’s SciencePal 2.0 self-evolving science agent. I was honored to receive the CityUHK Presidential PhD Scholarship.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from City University of Hong Kong in 2024, advised by Prof. Cong Wang; my M.S. in Control Science and Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2019, advised by Prof. Tao Zhang; and dual B.S. degrees in Automation and Mathematics from the Shen Yuan Honors College, Beihang University, in 2016.
